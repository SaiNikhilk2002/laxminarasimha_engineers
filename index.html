About the firm

Engineering that begins with the structure.

Laxmi Narasimha Engineers delivers structural engineering, design and project documentation solutions for residential, commercial and industrial developments.

Our work covers the engineering scope of a building project: structural systems for reinforced concrete and steel, layouts for individual sites and ventures, detailed engineering drawings for execution, and the estimation and tender documentation that supports procurement.

Every project is approached on its own terms. The structural approach is developed around the requirements of the site, the intended development and the way the project will actually be built.
